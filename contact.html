Contact

Tell us what you teach. Let's check the fit.

For a teacher message, share your classes, subjects, language you want replies in and Android phone model. For a school message, also include the rough number of teachers you would like to involve and the topics you want to try and check.

Privacy note: Please do not send student names, private records or answer papers in a first message.

What happens after you write

  • We check your topics, language and phone against the current version.

  • No fixed reply time or delivery date is promised.
