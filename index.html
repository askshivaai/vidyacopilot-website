What you are seeing

  1. Configure. Reply language: match the question, Hindi, Marathi or English. Speaker on or off.
  2. Ask. One complete question. Voice uses the phone's listening tool in the app.
  3. Reply. Written on the phone from what the AI has learned. The app has no internet permission.
  4. Read or listen. Speech can start before the reply is finished. Stop it any time.
  5. Review. The note under the reply says it came from memory, not your book.

“Can we trust replies before using them?”

Treat every reply as a first draft. Each one carries the note “Answered from memory, not from your book — worth checking.” The marking tool stays switched off until it is reliable enough. A small trial with clear goals is the honest way to judge it.

Reasons to choose it

Teacher control is part of the design.

Writes answers on the phone

The app writes replies on the Android phone itself. Android does not allow the app to connect to the internet.

Reminds you to check the reply

A note under the reply says it comes from the AI's memory, not your book. This reminds you to check it before use.

Fits the surroundings

Choose spoken replies or quiet reading. Stop speech when you need to interrupt.

Keeps the marking tool turned off for now

The tool that suggests marks is turned off in the regular app. It is not reliable enough yet.

The app can help. You still check and decide.

Trust

Clear about its limits from the first reply.

VIDYA Copilot can give wrong or unclear replies. Marathi needs more work. It is also less reliable on topics the AI has not learned well.

The regular teacher app does not look in your textbook before replying. It cannot currently suggest marks or read handwritten answer papers for everyday use.
