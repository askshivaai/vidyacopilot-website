What it does now

Helps in the small moments when you prepare.

Before a lesson

Go over a school topic

Ask about a topic you will teach. Use the reply as a start. Then check it.

While you walk

Listen when it suits you

Turn on spoken replies when listening is easier than reading. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.

In the staff room

Keep it quiet

Turn spoken replies off in the staff room or class. Then you do not disturb others.

Any time

Choose the reply language

Pick Hindi, Marathi or English. Or pick “Match the question” to get the reply in the language of your question.

“Can we trust the replies before we use them?”

Treat every reply as a first draft. Each one has the note “Answered from memory, not from your book — worth checking.” This means the app did not look at your textbook. The marking tool stays off until it is good enough. A small trial with clear goals is the honest way to judge it.

Can I trust it?

We tell you what it cannot do yet.

VIDYA Copilot can give wrong or confusing replies. Marathi replies need more work. Replies are also weaker on topics the AI has not learned well.

The regular teacher app does not look in your textbook before it replies. The tool that suggests marks is turned off for now. The app cannot read handwritten answer papers.
