About

Teachers need real help and clear limits.

With VIDYA Copilot, you can ask a question about a school topic. You can read or hear the reply. The app tells you what it cannot do. You decide what to use.

How we build it

The choices behind the app.

Work on the phone

The phone writes the reply itself. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.

Fit the school day

Type or speak. Choose to hear replies, or read them quietly.

Be honest about where replies come from

Replies today come from what the app's AI has learned. The app tells you this.

Keep improving each language

Offer Hindi, Marathi and English. Say clearly what still needs work.

Be careful with marking

Keep marking turned off until the tool is good enough.

Where we are

An early app, still proving itself.

Our reports cover early versions of the teacher app and their tests. This includes a newer version of the app’s AI on a test phone. They also say which ideas did not work and what still needs work.

We have not shown that many schools use it. We have not shown that it saves time or improves exam results. Small trials with teachers can help us learn if it is useful.
