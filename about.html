About

Teachers need useful support and clear boundaries.

VIDYA Copilot makes it easy to ask a question about a school topic. You can read or hear the reply. The app explains its limits, and you decide what to use.

Approach

The product choices behind that idea.

Work on the phone

The app writes answers on the phone. Voice uses the phone's separate listening and speaking tools.

Fit the school day

Let teachers type or speak, choose whether to hear replies and switch to quiet reading.

Be honest about the source

Current replies come from what the AI has learned. The app says so.

Keep improving the language experience

Offer Hindi, Marathi and English settings, and explain what still needs improvement.

Set a high bar for marking

Keep marking turned off until the tool is reliable enough.

Stage

An early product, still earning its place.

The supplied reports describe early teacher-app versions and tests, including a newer AI version on a test phone. They also explain which ideas failed and what still needs improvement.

We have not yet shown that many schools use it, that it saves a set amount of time or that it improves exam results. Small trials with teachers can help us learn how useful it is.
