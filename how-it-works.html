How it works

The app's AI is inside your phone.

VIDYA Copilot keeps its AI on your Android phone. You ask a school question. The phone writes the reply itself. The app itself cannot connect to the internet.

Check phones and languages Try the demo

Step by step

From your question to a reply.

01

Ask

Type a question. Or tap the microphone and speak. The phone's listening tool turns your words into text.

02

Get a reply

The phone writes the reply itself. It uses what the app's AI has learned. It does not look in your textbook.

03

Read or listen

If you turn on spoken replies, the phone reads the reply aloud as it is written.

04

Check

Check important facts in your textbook and notes. Then use the reply.

Internet and voice

Does it need internet?

The phone writes the reply itself, without internet. Voice is different. To hear you and to speak, the app uses the phone's own listening and speaking tools.

Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone. Turn internet off and try it before you depend on it.

We plan to add the app's own listening and speaking tools that work without internet. They are not in the app yet.

Inside the app
  • Your question, as text
  • The phone writes the reply itself
  • The reply and the reminder line
  • Your class, subject and language

The app is not allowed to use the internet.

Your phone's own tools
  • Listening tool (turns speech into text)
  • Speaking tool (reads text aloud)
  • Language files, if they are on the phone

These may use internet. They have their own privacy choices.

Speed

How fast does it start speaking?

In one test, with the app already ready, it began speaking about 434 ms (less than half a second) after the question was sent. This does not count the time spent listening to your question.

We also tested the newer AI version on an OPPO phone. These tests are not a promise. On your phone, a reply may take longer to start.

How to ask

Ask one question at a time.

The app treats each question as new. It does not remember your last question. So put all the details in each question.

Missing details“And what about its second stage?”
Has all details“What happens in the second stage of photosynthesis, for Class 7?”
