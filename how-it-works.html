How it works

The assistant's AI runs on your phone.

VIDYA Copilot uses AI saved on an Android phone to answer questions about school topics. The app itself cannot connect to the internet.

Check phones and languages Watch the walkthrough

Process

From question to reply.

01

Ask

Type a question or tap the microphone to speak. The phone's listening tool turns your speech into text.

02

Receive

The AI writes a reply on the phone using what it has learned. It does not look in your textbook.

03

Read or listen

If spoken replies are turned on, the phone reads the response aloud as it becomes available.

04

Review

Check important facts against your textbooks and teaching notes before using the reply.

Internet and voice

The app can write without internet. Voice needs a separate check.

The app writes answers on the phone. To hear your spoken question or read a reply aloud, it uses the phone's separate listening and speaking tools.

To use voice without internet, the phone needs the right language files. Try it with internet turned off before depending on it.

We plan to add the app's own tools for listening and speaking without internet. They are not included yet.

May use internet. Own privacy settings.

Speed

Designed to start speaking promptly, with results that vary by phone.

In one reported test, with the app already ready, it began speaking about 434 milliseconds after the question was sent, less than half a second. This did not include the time taken to listen to the question.

The newer AI version was also tested on an OPPO phone. These tests do not promise that every reply on every phone will start within a second.

Conversation design

Ask one question at a time.

Each question is treated as a new one. Add all the details needed for that question. Do not assume the app remembers an earlier reply.

Needs details“And what about its second stage?”
Complete“What happens in the second stage of photosynthesis, for Class 7?”
