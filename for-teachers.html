For teachers

Extra support for your questions. Respect for your knowledge.

You know your students and how to teach them. When you want to go over a school topic, VIDYA Copilot gives you another place to ask a question. You check the reply and choose what is useful.

Scenarios

Use it in the way your day allows.

Before a lesson

Ask about a topic you are preparing to explain. Review the reply alongside your teaching material.

Between classes

Ask a short question about a school topic. Include the details it needs. You do not need a long chat first.

In the staffroom

Read replies quietly with the speaker turned off.

When hands-free listening helps

Enable spoken replies. Stop speech whenever you need to move on.

In the language you prefer

Choose Hindi, Marathi or English, while allowing for the current language-quality gaps.

Example questions

Begin with a clear question.

  • “What is photosynthesis?”
  • “Explain equivalent fractions simply.”
  • “प्रकाशसंश्लेषण म्हणजे काय?”
  • “प्रकाश संश्लेषण क्या है?”

These examples show how you can ask. They do not promise a correct answer. Check whether the current app can help with your topics, and check any material before using it in class.

What to keep in mind

Keep it close to your textbooks and teaching notes.

Source

The app uses what its AI has learned. It does not look in your book before writing a reply.

Memory

The app treats every question as a new one. If the next question needs details from an earlier chat, type those details again.

Accuracy

Some replies may be wrong or hard to understand. Use them as a starting point while preparing, and check them before using them in class.

Next step

Start with a few topics you know well.

Start with a few questions you already know the answers to. Try them on your phone. Check whether the replies are useful, clear and in the right language.
