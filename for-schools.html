For schools

Try the app with teachers before using it across the school.

Start with your teachers' subjects and phones. Try a small set of questions to see whether the current VIDYA Copilot version is useful in your school.

Talk about a school trial Read the current limits

School questions

Check whether it is useful in your school.

Does it cover your topics?

Current work focuses especially on Classes 6–8 topics. Confirm your intended classes and subjects.

Is the language right for you?

Test the languages teachers use. Marathi quality remains a known area for improvement.

Does it work on the phone?

Try the app on the phones your teachers will use. Check how quickly it replies and whether voice works. Results from one test phone may be different.

Teacher review

The app answers from what its AI has learned and can make mistakes. Teachers should check each reply before using it in class.

Are the features you need available?

Do not depend on it to suggest marks or read handwritten papers. These features are not available in the regular app.

Suggested trial steps

A manageable way to begin.

1

Choose a small group of teachers and a few topics.

Decide what you want to learn from the trial.

2

Check how to get the app and which phones to use.

Discuss the app version, setup steps and the phones available.

3

Review actual replies.

Ask teachers to compare replies with their books and notes. Are the facts right? Is the explanation clear? Is the language suitable?

4

Check everyday controls.

Try voice, quiet mode, stopping speech and asking separate questions in the places teachers will use the app.

5

Decide from the findings.

Use the results to decide whether to continue, try fewer topics or teachers, or wait for improvements.

These are suggested steps for a school trial. They do not mean a school programme is already running or that time savings have been proved.

Privacy and what is included

The current teacher app runs separately.

Replies on the phone

VIDYA Copilot writes replies on the phone, and the app itself cannot use internet. The phone's separate listening and speaking tools may still use internet.

No school system link

The teacher app does not currently connect to a school information page or the student app's school system. A ready-to-use service showing class study reports is not available.

No answer papers, please

Do not send student answer papers in your first message. Any testing of marking tools needs a separate agreement and checks on how the papers will be used.
