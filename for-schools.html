Questions for your school

Is it useful in your school?

Does it cover your topics?

Right now we work mostly on Classes 6–8 topics. Tell us which classes and subjects you want.

Is the language right for you?

Try the languages your teachers use. We know Marathi replies still need to get better.

Does it work on your phones?

Try the app on the phones your teachers will use. Check how fast it replies and if voice works. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone. Your phones may differ from our test phone.

Who checks the replies?

The app replies from what its AI has learned. It can make mistakes. Teachers should check each reply before using it in class.

Does it do what you need?

It cannot suggest marks or read handwritten papers. These are not in the regular app.

Steps for a small trial

An easy way to start.

1

Choose a few teachers and a few topics.

Decide what you want to find out from the small trial.

2

Find out how to get the app and which phones to use.

Talk with us about the app version, how to set it up and your phones.

3

Check real replies.

Ask teachers to compare replies with their books and notes. Are the facts right? Is it clear? Is the language right?

4

Try the everyday buttons.

Try voice, reading quietly, stopping the voice and asking new questions. Do this where teachers will use the app.

5

Decide from what you found.

You can carry on, try fewer topics or teachers, or wait until the app gets better.

These are only suggested steps for a small trial. They do not mean a school is already using it this way. We have not shown that it saves time.

Privacy and what is in the app

The teacher app works on its own.

Replies on the phone

The phone writes the reply itself. The app itself cannot connect to the internet. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.

Not linked to school systems

The teacher app is not linked to any school records page. It is not linked to the student app's school system. There are no ready reports on how a class is doing.

No answer papers, please

Do not send student answer papers in your first message. Testing any marking tool needs a separate agreement first. We must also agree how the papers will be used.
