Features

Simple controls for a teacher's school day.

Ask a question in the way that suits you. Choose how to receive the reply. Review it before using it.

See how to get the app Try the simulation

Ask by typing or voice.

Available

Type when you want to write the question carefully. Tap the microphone when you prefer speaking. The app uses the phone's listening tool, so voice may work differently on different phones and may need internet.

Hear a reply while it is being written.

Available · speed varies

Reading replies aloud can begin before the whole reply is finished. You can listen as the answer appears instead of waiting for a complete block of text. This feature is available, but its speed can differ from phone to phone.

Stop speech when you need to interrupt.

Available

Use the stop control to end a spoken reply. You remain in charge of when to listen and when to move on.

Switch to quiet mode.

Available

Turn “Speak replies aloud” off for a quiet staffroom or whenever you prefer reading.

Set your reply language.

Available · quality varies

Choose Hindi, Marathi or English, or ask the app to match the question. Some replies may still use the wrong language, mix different language letters or be unclear.

See the reminder to check the reply.

Always shown

The regular app currently says: “Answered from memory, not from your book — worth checking.” This message tells you how the reply was written. The feature that adds textbook facts is turned off in the regular app.

Keep your teacher profile on the phone.

Saved on the phone

Your profile includes your name, school, classes and subjects. It is saved on the phone. This teacher app does not currently link to the school connection used by the separate student test app.

Real app screen reserved.

New design preview · Ask which version you will get

A simpler home screen is in the new design being tested.

The new design includes a greeting, class and subject choices, starter questions, a large microphone and clearer status messages.
