Website privacy note

What this website collects.

This page describes the website only. It is written from how the site is actually built. It is not a legal privacy policy; approved legal text will be added when checked company details are supplied.

Forms and messages

The website has no contact form. Every “Ask how to get the app” button opens your own email app with a message starter. Nothing is sent through this website, and the website does not know whether you sent the message.

Cookies and analytics

The website sets no cookies and loads no third-party analytics or advertising scripts. A small event layer records interactions such as button clicks and video plays inside your browser only, so a privacy-respecting analytics tool can be attached later. Until one is configured, those events leave nothing behind.

The simulation

The interface simulation on the home and demo pages runs entirely in your browser. It does not record audio, does not send your typed text anywhere and has no connection to the app.

Fonts, images and video

Fonts, mascot images, animation clips and the brochure are served from this website, not from third-party font or video services.

The app itself

The teacher app writes replies on the phone and has no internet permission. The phone's separate listening and speaking tools may use the internet and have their own privacy settings. Read Trust and teacher control for the plain-language explanation.
