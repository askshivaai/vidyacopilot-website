For a school message, include the rough number of teachers trying the app.

Expectations

What should I expect from the current version?

What you get

You can type or speak a question, read or hear the reply, and choose a reply language. The app reminds you that it does not look in your textbook before answering.

Which version

The installed app may look different from the new design being tested. Ask which version you will try.

Not included

The regular app does not include marking, handwritten-paper reading or ready-to-use school study reports.
