About the app and its name

What is VIDYA Copilot?

It is an Android phone app for teachers. You ask a school question by typing or speaking. You can read the reply or hear it aloud. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.

Why does the app say VIDYA Shikshak?

Right now the teacher app shows the name VIDYA Shikshak. This website calls the same app VIDYA Copilot. You may still see Shikshak when you open the app.

Is this the same as the VIDYA AI student app?

No. This website is for the teacher app. VIDYA AI is a different app, made for students. The student app looks in the books it has. The regular teacher app uses what its AI has learned. It does not search your textbook.

Who is it for?

It is for school teachers in India, mainly those who teach Classes 6–8. It is also for schools that want to try it with their teachers. First check that it covers the topics you need.

Replies, languages and internet

Does it answer from my textbook?

Not in the regular teacher app. The part that uses facts from the textbook is turned off. Replies come from what the app's AI has learned. The app reminds you to check them.

Can I use Hindi, Marathi or English?

Yes. You can pick Hindi, Marathi or English. “Match the question” means the app replies in the language you asked in. It may still use the wrong language, or give a reply that is not clear. Marathi replies still need more work.

Does it need internet?

The phone writes the reply itself. The app itself cannot connect to the internet. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.

Will voice work without internet?

It depends on your phone and the language files on it. Language files are extra downloads that let your phone listen and speak without internet. Turn internet off and try voice before you depend on it.

Can I switch speech off?

Yes. Turn “Speak replies aloud” off. Then you can read the reply quietly.

Can I stop a spoken reply?

Yes. Use the stop control while the app is speaking the reply.

Does it remember my earlier question?

No. It treats every question as a new one. Give all the details each time, even if you said them before.

Are replies always correct?

No. Replies can be wrong, unclear or hard to make sense of. Check important parts against your textbooks and teaching notes before you use them in class.

Does it always answer in under a second?

Not always. In one test, with the app already ready, it began speaking about 434 milliseconds (less than half a second) after the question was sent. This was one test on one phone, not a promise. Other phones and questions may take longer. This time did not include listening to the question.

Marking and reports

Can it mark answer papers?

A marking tool has been built for testing. It is turned off in the regular app. It is not good enough yet, so you cannot use it for everyday marking.

Can it read handwritten answers?

Not yet. We tested reading handwritten papers without internet. The results were not good enough, so this is not in the app.

Will AI decide a student's final marks?

The tool we are building is meant to suggest marks. The teacher makes the final decision. This tool is turned off in the regular app today.

Does it give class study reports or a school information page?

No. The current app has no ready page that shows class study reports. These reports are planned for later.

Phones, price and getting the app

Does it work on every low-cost phone?

We cannot promise that yet. We have not finished testing on low-cost phones. Please check the phone you plan to use.

Is there an iPhone version?

An iPhone version is not confirmed. The current app is for Android phones.

Is it free?

We cannot say yet if teachers will need to pay. Prices have not been announced. Ask us how to get the app and if there are any charges.

Where can I download it?

It is not on the Play Store yet. Contact us to ask how to set it up. We can also tell you if this version can help with the topics you teach.

Can our school arrange a trial?

Yes, you can ask about a small trial. First we need to talk about the topics, the phones, and how teachers will check the replies. A trial may not be ready straight away.
