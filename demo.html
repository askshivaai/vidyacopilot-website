Try the demo

Tap a question. Watch the reply appear. Read the reminder.

This is a drawing of the app made for this website – not a real screen. It shows the choices the app offers: reply language, “Speak replies aloud”, the stop button and the reminder that the reply came from memory.

  • Choose a reply language, or let it match the language of your question.
  • Turn speaking off to see quiet mode. Turn it on and press Stop while it speaks.
  • Type anything: the demo only has hand-written replies for the sample questions. It tells you this.
