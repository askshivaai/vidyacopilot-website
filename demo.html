Try the simulation

Tap a question. Watch the reply arrive. Notice the warning.

This is an interface simulation built for the website. It shows the controls the current app offers: reply language, “Speak replies aloud”, the stop control and the reminder that the reply came from memory.

  • Choose a reply language or let it match the question.
  • Switch speech off to see quiet mode. Switch it on and press Stop mid-reply.
  • Type anything: the simulation only has hand-written sample replies for the starter questions, and says so.

Sample replies were written by hand for this demonstration. They are not output from the app and do not show how accurate the app is.
