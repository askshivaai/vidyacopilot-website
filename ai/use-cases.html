VIDYA Copilot: when to use it

WhenWhat you doWhat you get and check
Before a lesson, going over a topic you knowAsk one full question, by typing or speakingA first reply to check against the textbook and your notes
Between classes, a quick questionPut the class, topic and details in one questionA reply to read or hear. No long chat needed.
Quiet staff roomTurn "Speak replies aloud" offRead quietly without disturbing others
Hands busy, you want to listenTurn speaking on. Stop it when you need to.The phone's speaking tool reads the reply. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.
Teaching in Marathi or HindiChoose the reply language, or pick "Match the question"A reply in that language. Check that it is clear and in the right script. Marathi needs more work.

By who is asking

WhoTheir questionHonest answer
Class teacherWill it help with the small questions in my day?Yes, one question at a time. The teacher checks each reply.
Teacher in a Marathi-medium schoolCan I use the language I like?Marathi is there. Our tests found Marathi needed the most work.
Academic coordinator (who guides teaching in a school)Can we trust replies before using them?Treat every reply as a first draft. Every reply shows the memory reminder. Marking is turned off.
Principal or school leaderCan we try it carefully?Yes: a few teachers, topics they know, their real phones, and teachers checking replies. Then decide from what you find.

What it cannot do today

  • Marking answer papers (turned off).
  • Reading handwritten papers (not available).
  • Class study reports or a school summary page (planned).
  • Replies checked against your textbook (turned off).
  • Use by students (that is the separate VIDYA AI app).
