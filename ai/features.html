VIDYA Copilot: what it does

Works now (regular app)

  • Type a school question.
  • Speak your question. The phone's listening tool hears it. This may not work the same on every phone. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.
  • Hear the reply. Speaking can start before the reply is finished. Speed is different on each phone.
  • A stop control to end a spoken reply.
  • Quiet reading. Turn "Speak replies aloud" off.
  • Reply language: Hindi, Marathi, English or match the question. Quality varies. It may reply in the wrong language or mix scripts (ways of writing).
  • Reminder under each reply: "Answered from memory, not from your book — worth checking."
  • Your details saved on the phone.
  • One question at a time. It does not remember earlier questions.

Built but turned off in the regular app

  • Using textbook facts to write replies. Finding the topic was tested, but adding facts did not improve replies enough.
  • Suggested marks. It checks a typed student answer against a marking guide and shows the words that support the mark. Not good enough yet.

Planned or still being worked on (no release dates)

  • Better language and better replies, especially in Marathi.
  • The app's own listening and speaking tools, so voice does not depend on the phone's tools.
  • Suggested marks that teachers check. The teacher decides the final marks.
  • Reading handwritten answers, only when it works well without internet.
  • Class study reports.
  • More tests on phones, including low-cost phones. Checks with screen readers (tools that read the screen aloud).

A new screen we are testing

It has a greeting, class and subject choices, starter questions, a large microphone and clearer messages. Tapping a starter question puts it in the box. Nothing is sent until the teacher sends it. The app on your phone may show the regular screen or the new one.
