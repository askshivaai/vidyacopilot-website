VIDYA Copilot: app fact sheet

ItemDetails
App name (on this website)VIDYA Copilot
App name (on the phone)VIDYA Shikshak
What it isAndroid phone app for teachers' school questions
PhonesAndroid only. iPhone not confirmed.
How to get itEarly version. Not on the Play Store. Contact the team to get it.
PriceNot announced. No plan, monthly price or school package is confirmed yet.
How you askType or speak (the phone's listening tool)
What you getA written reply. You can also hear it (the phone's speaking tool). Speaking can start before the reply is finished, and you can stop it. Listening and speaking use your phone's own tools. These may need internet unless the right language files are on your phone.
Reply languagesHindi, Marathi, English, or match the question. Quality varies. Marathi needs the most work.
Where the reply is writtenOn the phone. The app is not allowed to use the internet.
Where replies come fromWhat the app's AI has learned. No textbook search in the regular app.
Reminder on every reply"Answered from memory, not from your book — worth checking."
Remembers earlier questionsNo. Each question is treated as new.
Your detailsName, school, classes, subjects. Saved on the phone. Not linked to any school system.
TopicsMainly Classes 6–8. Full topic list not shared yet.
Speed in one testBegan speaking about 434 ms (less than half a second) after the question was sent. One test, app already ready, OPPO phone. Listening time not included. Not a promise.
Reply quality in one testAbout 1 in 10 replies were hard to make sense of, in one phone test of the newer version.
MarkingBuilt for testing. Turned off in the regular app.
Reading handwritingNot available. Tests were not good enough.
Class study reportsPlanned. Not available.
Checking against your textbookBuilt. Turned off. It did not improve replies enough.
New screenBeing tested: greeting, class and subject choices, starter questions, large microphone, clearer messages.
